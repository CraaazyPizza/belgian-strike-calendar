About the data

Transport disruption covers reported interruptions to rail or public transport networks, flights or shipping. The demonstration view shows dates with a reported turnout of at least 1,000. Student actions include school pupils and university students. An event can appear in several views.

Sources include unions, employers, parliament, transport operators and news reports in Dutch, French, German and English. Some local strikes have no public report. A blank day has no matching entry.

A national strike and a factory that joined it can each have an entry. For working days lost, see the ONSS quarterly figures or the 2025 estimate.

Dates follow Belgian time. An overnight strike can cover two dates. During a long strike, different sites can be on strike on different days. For open-ended notices, the calendar usually marks the opening date.

Rally or other actions include rallies, blockades and work-to-rule. A call or notice can include a reduced-service plan. Some days have more than one type of action. The small marks show which ones.

Regional filters include nationwide actions. A Brussels march can draw participants from across Wallonia or Flanders. The event lists the meeting place. ACOD and CGSP are the Dutch and French names of the same public-service union.
